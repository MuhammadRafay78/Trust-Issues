Trust Issues: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Trust Issues is a single-player game that works offline. This page explains what the app does with your information.

What we collect

We do not run any servers, accounts or analytics, and we do not collect or store personal information ourselves. The only information that leaves your device is what the advertising service and Google Play handle, described below.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: the furthest level reached, your star ratings, the hearts you collected, the cosmetic items you own and the ones you are wearing. This is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it.

Ads

The game can show an optional rewarded video ad, only when you choose to watch one to skip a level. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When you watch an ad, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and device and usage information to serve and measure ads. In regions where the law requires it (such as the EEA and UK), you will first be shown a consent form from Google and can choose whether ads are personalised. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See Google's advertising policies for details.

Purchases

You can buy cosmetic items (colours and hats for the mascot). Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google tells the app which items you own so that you can restore them on a new device.

Network access

The game works fully offline. It only uses the internet to show an ad you asked for or to complete or restore a purchase.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files. It uses the internet (for ads and purchases) and Google Play billing.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the game later changes how information is handled, this page will be updated before the change is released, and the date above will change.
