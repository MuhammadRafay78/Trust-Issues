Trust Issues: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Trust Issues is a single-player offline game. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects nothing.

What we collect

We do not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

The game saves one number on your device: the furthest level you have reached. This is stored locally so that "Continue" works. It is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it.

Network access

The game works fully offline. It does not load fonts, images, scripts or any other content from the internet, and it does not contact any server.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the game later adds features that change how information is handled (for example ads or online leaderboards), this page will be updated before the change is released, and the date above will change.
